FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kind of videos can you edit?

Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts, promotional videos, product videos, restaurant content, café content and other short-form social media videos.

Do I need professional camera footage?

No. I can work with footage recorded on a phone or camera. Good editing can help organize and polish the footage you already have.

How do I send the footage?

We can use a file-sharing service or another convenient method to transfer the raw footage.

How long does an edit take?

Turnaround depends on the amount and complexity of the footage, but fast turnaround can be arranged for suitable projects.

Can you follow a specific style?

Yes. You can send references, examples or a general direction and the edit can be built around that style.
